A character-bible silhouette lock sheet on pale textured parchment paper. High-contrast graphic design, not an illustration.

Two figures as SOLID FILLED black paper cutouts standing on a thin ground line. Interiors are completely opaque black. No faces, no eyes, no cloth folds, no armor highlights, no gray shading, no white holes inside the black shapes, no stipple.

Left: a shorter woman, full body. Long hair falling down her back, not a headscarf. A cloak over the shoulders. A blocky breastplate and shoulder-armor mass. A full floor-length heavy skirt. She holds a small candle at chest height with both hands. The only color is a tiny orange-yellow candle flame.

Right and slightly behind: a taller standing knight in full plate, closed visor helmet, a large plume rising from the helm and curving back. Clearly taller than the woman. Standing guard, not kneeling.

Centered title text: VIOLET OATH LOCK O-3. Labels VIOLET and OATHKEEPER under the ground line. A tiny 64 thumbnail of the same pair at the bottom right.

Clean ink silhouettes, production concept-art sheet. No anime painting, no photoreal metal, no extra people, no wings, no horns, no scenery.
