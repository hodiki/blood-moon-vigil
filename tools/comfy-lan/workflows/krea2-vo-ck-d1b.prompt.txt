A character-bible color-key lock sheet on pale textured parchment paper. High-contrast graphic design, not an illustration.

Two figures as SOLID FILLED paper-cut stamps standing on a thin ground line. Each body is opaque screenprint color regions, 5 to 6 flat fills per figure. No faces, no eyes, no noses, no cloth folds, no armor specular highlights, no painterly shading, no gray gradients, no white holes inside the shapes, no stipple, no mosaic.

Left: a shorter woman, full body, near-frontal. Long hair falling down her back, not a headscarf. A cloak over the shoulders. A blocky breastplate and shoulder-armor mass. A full floor-length heavy skirt. She holds a small candle at chest height with both hands.

Right and slightly behind: a taller standing knight in full plate, closed visor helmet, a large plume rising from the helm and curving back. Clearly taller than the woman. Standing guard, not kneeling. Hip sword block.
